About Us

Our story, our mission, and the man who inspired it all

Our Foundation

The Qasem Ghairat Foundation is a non-profit philanthropic organization established to honor and continue the life's work of Dr. Mohammad Qasem Ghairat — a man who devoted himself entirely to the cause of education across Afghanistan and Pakistan.

We believe, as Dr. Ghairat believed, that education is not a luxury for the few but a fundamental right for every person. Our work is guided by this conviction, and everything we do flows from it.

The Legacy of Dr. Mohammad Qasem Ghairat

Dr. Mohammad Qasem Ghairat was not a man who chose education as a career — he chose it as a calling. Throughout his life, he served communities across Afghanistan and Pakistan, tirelessly working to expand access to learning for those who needed it most.

His guiding motto — "every person should get educated, no matter what and when" — was not merely a slogan. It was the organizing principle of his life, the lens through which he made every decision, and the legacy he left behind.

The Qasem Ghairat Foundation was created to ensure that legacy lives on — that the work he started continues, and that future generations in Afghanistan and Pakistan have the educational opportunities they deserve.

Our Mission

To make quality education accessible to all people — particularly those in underserved communities in Afghanistan and Pakistan — regardless of gender, socioeconomic background, or circumstance.

Our Values

  • Education as a right — Every person, everywhere, deserves access to learning.
  • Unconditional service — We serve without judgment, without barriers, without exception.
  • Community first — We work with communities, not just for them.
  • Integrity — We are transparent, accountable, and responsible stewards of every contribution.
  • Lasting impact — We invest in sustainable change, not short-term solutions.

Who We Serve

Our programs focus primarily on communities in Afghanistan and Pakistan where access to quality education is limited by poverty, conflict, displacement, or systemic inequality. We are particularly committed to expanding educational access for girls and women, who face disproportionate barriers in these regions.
